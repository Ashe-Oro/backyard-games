# Arcade audio direction

Keep the sound bed quiet and local: scattered birds, soft breeze/leaves, distant
conversation, occasional cup clinks and keg pours. Avoid intelligible repeated
speech. Duck ambience/music for bag impacts and hole celebrations when effects
are integrated. Keep each layer separately adjustable; do not fill every second.

  Audition grass footsteps, gentle wood hits and light metal/cup-like taps.
  Official licensing: https://kenney.nl/support . Keep the included license.

  source for a later curated soundtrack; check the selected track's terms and
  include required credit, or obtain its appropriate paid license. No music
  from this catalog is bundled.

Music direction: relaxed instrumental acoustic funk, light blues or easygoing
lo-fi, without vocals, hard drops or constant arcade jingles. A small rotating
set should feel like a cookout radio, not a competitive countdown.

## Local playlist implemented

Pause menu → MUSIC. Choose a folder, or put files in the default location and
select REFRESH. Default on Batocera: `/userdata/music/backyard-cornhole`.
Windows/macOS default: `Backyard Cornhole` inside the user's Music folder.
Supported: MP3, OGG, WAV, M4A. Top-level files play in natural filename order
(e.g. `01 - …`, `02 - …`) and loop through the list. PLAY/PAUSE, NEXT TRACK and
volume use the existing menu navigation/buttons. Folder and volume persist.
Playback is offline; users supply their own tracks. No commercial songs ship.
Files over 100 MB are skipped; unreadable/unplayable tracks are skipped, with a
bounded stop if none can play. File changes require REFRESH.

Sound effects/ambience above are a researched shortlist, not shipped recordings.
Audio playback still needs a cabinet listening test; automated decoding is not
an assessment of music taste, loudness balance or speaker quality.
