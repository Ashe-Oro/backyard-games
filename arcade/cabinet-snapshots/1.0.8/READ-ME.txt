Backyard Cornhole Arcade — 1.0.8
================================

PLAY
Roll forward to throw. Roll faster for more power; a long slow roll stays soft.
Aim where you roll: 1 left, 3 right.
Backswing is optional: back-left spins right, back-right spins left.

BUTTONS
TOP     HEIGHT · SURFACE · RESET · CLEAR
BOTTOM  LEFT · RIGHT · RESET · KEYS
The top-right red button clears the board in Practice and keeps your stats.
The bottom-right button tucks or pops KEYS; your choice is remembered. SURFACE switches
SLICK / STICKY. The tiny readout shows your current height and surface.
Height cycles Kobe / Slider / Normal. Use the bottom buttons to line up;
sideways rolls do not move the bag. RESET centers your bag and clears windup.

PICK A GAME
Practice, share the trackball in 2 PLAYER, or take on eight tournament rivals.
Pause to resume, change music, or open CABINET SETUP.
Keyboard: H height, F surface, R reset, Enter select, Escape pause, F2 setup.

SETUP
Adjust FULL POWER or choose CALIBRATE TRACKBALL.
Roll forward hard, like a full-power throw.
RESET SETTINGS restores the starting setup.

NEW THIS ROUND
The big power bar is gone. One compact arrow from your bag shows direction and power.
A short arrow is a soft toss; a long, filled arrow is full power. The faint guide
shows how much farther it can fill. Lean left or right to aim.
It stays about two seconds after release, then fades.
Line up left or right and roll straight: your bag stays on that side of the hole.
Straight throws no longer auto-aim at the hole. Aim and backswing hooks still work.
The CLEAR BOARD popup is gone. Top-right clears the board in Practice.
Only bottom-right tucks the keys legend.
Your running version is shown in the upper-right corner during play.
The blue cooler has been removed from the yard.
The vertical power bar fills with your throw power.
It holds for about two seconds after your throw, then hides.
A full-power roll is now a bit easier to reach.

SPINNER
Pull back toward A for clockwise spin and a right hook; C spins left.
Roll forward toward 1 to aim left or 3 to aim right. Straight back: no spin.
SLICK carries a longer hook; STICKY stops sooner. See the small left legend.

A soft glow around your bag means you can roll. If you roll before your turn,
let the spinner stop, then roll again for a fresh toss.
